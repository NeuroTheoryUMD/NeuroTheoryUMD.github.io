NeuroAI: interpreting deep neural networks through the lens of biological processing

Deep neural networks can now solve many visual tasks, and often do so with internal representations that resemble those of the brain. Yet without principles for interpreting what these networks compute, we understand neither biological nor artificial computation. On the one hand, we use machine learning tools to capture the computations performed within populations of recorded neurons, and develop methods to link them to cellular and circuit mechanisms. On the other hand, we look to how artificial systems solve complex tasks to understand how computations might be structured in biological systems.
